# Privacy Policy — Idle Empire

**App:** Idle Empire (`com.idleempire.tycoon`)
**Effective:** 6 September 2026

**Short version:** Idle Empire collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no internet access. Your
game progress is saved only on your own device.

## Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect, transmit, or store any personal information. There is no
account, no sign-in, no email capture, and no user profile of any kind.

## Information we share

None. Because no information is collected, there is nothing to share, sell, or disclose to any
third party.

## Network access

The app requests no Android permissions at all, including no `INTERNET` permission. It cannot make
network connections. There are no analytics SDKs, no crash reporting services, no advertising SDKs,
and no advertising identifier is read or used.

## Data stored on your device

Your game progress is written to the app's own private storage on your device. It contains:

- how many of each generator you own, and which upgrades you have bought;
- your in-game currency, lifetime earnings and prestige points;
- your total tap count and the time the game was last closed, used to calculate offline earnings.

This save data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently, and it cannot
be recovered by us because we never had a copy.

## Advertising and purchases

Idle Empire contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. No payment information is ever
requested or processed.

## Children

The app is rated for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Since no personal
information is collected from anyone, no information is knowingly collected from children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy — for example, if analytics or ads were added
in a future version — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the
effective date above will change.
