Privacy Policy

Idle Empire (com.idleempire.tycoon) · Effective 11 September 2026 (rev. 3 - optional rewarded ads added)

Short version: Idle Empire shows a banner ad, and full-screen reward ads only when you tap an offer to watch one, all through Google AdMob. Google collects data through those ads, including your device's advertising ID. We do not collect, receive or store any personal information ourselves, and your game progress stays on your own device.

Information we collect

We collect nothing ourselves. There is no account, no sign-in, no email capture and no user profile. We operate no server and receive no data about you.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays a banner advertisement supplied by Google AdMob. It can also show a full-screen rewarded advertisement, but only after you tap an offer that names the reward (for example doubling offline earnings); you can always decline and keep playing. To select and measure those ads, Google's advertising SDK collects information directly from your device, which typically includes:

  • your device's advertising ID (a resettable identifier);
  • device and app information such as model, operating system version, language, and the app you are using;
  • coarse location derived from your IP address;
  • interactions with the ads themselves, such as views and clicks.

That data goes to Google, not to us. Google's use of it is described in its Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

You can limit ad personalisation at any time in Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads, where you can also delete or reset your advertising ID. Doing so does not remove the ads, only their personalisation.

Consent

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message the first time it runs, and your choice controls whether you are shown personalised or non-personalised ads. You can reopen that choice by clearing the app's data and restarting it.

Network access

The app requests the INTERNET permission and access to the advertising ID solely so the advertising SDK can fetch and display ads. The game itself does not send your progress, your taps or any other gameplay information anywhere. There is no analytics SDK and no crash reporting service.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress is written to the app's own private storage on your device. It contains:

  • how many of each generator you own, and which upgrades you have bought;
  • your in-game currency, lifetime earnings and prestige points;
  • your total tap count and the time the game was last closed, used to calculate offline earnings.

This save data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently, and it cannot be recovered by us because we never had a copy.

Purchases

Idle Empire has no in-app purchases. No payment information is ever requested or processed.

Children

The app is rated for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and you want their advertising ID reset, that can be done from the Android settings described above.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy - for example if another advertising network, analytics or in-app purchases were added - this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
